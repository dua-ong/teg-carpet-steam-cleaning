The problem

Cold cleaning has limits.

Light vacuuming and mopping keep things looking okay, but they don't always reach embedded soil or help with hygiene the way heat extraction can.

Steam and hot-water methods are often recommended when you want a deeper reset of carpets and suitable hard surfaces.
