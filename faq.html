Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know before booking, answered honestly.

About Our Services

What services does T.E.G offer?

We offer carpet cleaning, tile and grout cleaning, upholstery / couch cleaning, steam cleaning, carpet stain removal, commercial carpet cleaning, area rug cleaning, pet odor & stain removal, and more. We serve both homes and businesses across Greater Milwaukee.

Do you clean commercial properties as well as homes?

Yes. We work with offices, retail stores, restaurants, and other commercial spaces. We can schedule cleanings outside of business hours to minimize disruption.

Can you clean both carpet and hard floors in the same visit?

Yes. Many clients schedule multiple services in the same appointment — for example carpet cleaning in the bedrooms and tile cleaning in the kitchen or bathrooms.

Pricing & Booking

How much does carpet cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the area, the service type, and the condition of the carpet. We provide a clear, honest quote before we start any work. There are no hidden fees.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Requesting a quote from T.E.G costs nothing and comes with no obligation to book.

How do I book an appointment?

You can request a quote through our online form or call us directly at +1 (414) 775-3705. We’ll confirm the details and schedule a time that works for you.

Safety & Products

Are your cleaning products safe for children and pets?

Yes. We use professional-grade solutions that are effective on soil and stains, and safe for children and pets once dry. If anyone in your household has specific allergies or chemical sensitivities, let us know when you book and we’ll adjust accordingly.

Do you use steam cleaning or hot water extraction?

We use hot water extraction (often called steam cleaning by customers), the method most carpet manufacturers recommend for deep cleaning.

Before & After the Appointment

Do I need to move furniture before you arrive?

We move standard furniture (sofas, chairs, coffee tables, dining chairs). We ask that you pre-move breakables, electronics, plants, and very heavy items like pianos, beds, and large armoires.

How long does carpet cleaning take to dry?

Most carpet dries within 4 to 8 hours depending on carpet thickness, humidity, and airflow. We recommend opening windows and running fans to speed drying. Avoid walking on wet carpet with shoes.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

We ask that an adult (18+) be present at the start of the appointment to confirm the scope, pricing, and access. After that walkthrough, it’s up to you whether to stay or step out.

Specific Situations

Can you remove old stains that have set in for months?

Many set-in stains can be significantly improved or removed with professional treatment. We’ll assess the stain and give you an honest answer before we start.

Can pet odor be fully removed from carpet?

It depends on the extent of contamination. Surface odors respond well to professional cleaning. If urine has soaked into the padding or subfloor, deeper treatment is required. We’ll tell you honestly what to expect.
